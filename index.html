🔧 If Something Does Not Work

Most hiccups have a simple cause, and a simple fix.

  • Nothing happens when you double-click the file. Right-click it and choose the option to run it as an administrator. This clears up most permission-related stalls.
  • The download crawls or stops. Pause it, wait a few seconds, and start it again. Switching from Wi-Fi to a wired connection often makes a dramatic difference for large files.
  • Your PS5 is not found. Confirm the console and the computer are on the same network. A guest network or a separate Wi-Fi name will keep them apart even when they are in the same room.
  • The install finishes but nothing appears. Give the home screen a moment to refresh, then look through your full game list rather than only the most recent row.
  • Windows shows a blue warning screen. Click the "More info" text and then the button that lets the program run. New programs commonly trigger this the first time.
  • Everything feels stuck. Close the program completely, open it again, and repeat your last action. A fresh start resolves the vast majority of odd behaviour.
